Our story

We took a different path

And that is rather the point. Behind Merywood are 8 years of experience in the supplements and cosmetics industry. As a company, Merywood was established 4 years ago, but the expertise came first, built on a simple observation about a fast-moving market: brands with strong ideas kept getting stuck on the hard part, turning a concept into a compliant, market-ready product. Formulation, sourcing, EU paperwork, packaging, every piece sat with a different supplier, and the brand owner was left to hold it all together.

So we did it differently. We brought the whole journey together under one partner, from first formula to finished, EU-ready product, and we moved fast: reacting to what the market wanted, developing new formulations, and building the kind of flexible, service-first partnership that legacy manufacturers rarely offer.

We work across the European market. Our company is registered in Estonia, our products are made in the EU, and our partners sell to customers throughout Europe.

We made one choice on purpose: Merywood never competes with the brands we build. Your product stays yours, your customers stay yours, and the growth is yours to keep.

Our values

What we hold to

01

Quality first

Every product we make carries our client’s name and reputation, not just our work. We hold a consistent standard across reliable ingredients, careful production, and repeatable results, batch after batch.

02

Safety and compliance

EU rules on documentation, labeling, and registration are strict. We treat that as our job, not yours: we prepare the paperwork, check every requirement, and stay on the details, so your product reaches the market cleanly and your business carries no hidden regulatory risk.

03

Transparency and trust

We build long-term partnerships through clear communication and honest processes, with full support at every stage of product creation. No surprises, no hidden steps.

04

Flexibility

Every brand has its own goals, audience, and market. We adapt our solutions to fit different product concepts, regulatory environments, and business sizes, rather than forcing one model on everyone.

05

Market expertise

We work across European supplement and cosmetics markets every day. We know what sells, what regulators expect, and how a product needs to be built to compete, and that knowledge goes into every project.

06

Innovation

We track market trends and develop new formulations continuously, so our partners launch products that compete on more than price.
