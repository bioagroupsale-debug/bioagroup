How it works

Client Consultation

We begin by aligning with your brand identity and product goals. Whether you’re launching a new skincare line, expanding your beauty brand, or adding to your personal care range, we’ll define your ideal formula, target market, and product type. Together, we’ll explore ingredient selection, formulations, and packaging solutions that reflect your vision.

Research & Development

Our R&D team formulates a custom blend using carefully selected ingredients to meet your desired skin benefits and market needs. We focus on product quality, stability, and performance, refining the formula as needed to ensure the highest quality standards for your finished product.

Trial Production & Testing

We produce a small initial batch to fine-tune both the formula and manufacturing process. During this stage, we manage any material loss, verify aesthetic consistency, and run in-depth quality control checks, ensuring the product performs across different skin types and aligns with your private label skincare expectations.

Process Optimization

After the trial run, we assess and refine the production process to improve efficiency, reduce waste, and ensure consistent quality across all future batches.

Regulatory Support

Our team takes care of EU regulatory compliance, including product registration, safety documentation, and labeling guidance. We ensure your private label cosmetics are fully prepared for legal distribution and entry into the cosmetics market.

Production

Once finalized, your products move into full-scale manufacturing.
With expertise in white label and private label solutions, we make it easy to create standout beauty products that align with your values, meet market standards, and support your business growth, with a focus on sustainability, eco-friendly practices, and premium quality.
