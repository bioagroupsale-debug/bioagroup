Private label pet supplements

Choose the format that fits your category, margin model, and customer routine

Best for:

  • Building a custom formulation around your goals (daily wellness, breed or age focus, category differentiation)
  • Choosing the right format: capsules, tablets, powders, liquids, soft chews, or lick paste
  • Aligning packaging and labelling with your market requirements
  • Managing documentation and compliance under the same standards we apply across our supplements manufacturing
  • Registering your own company and own label

White label pet supplements

White label is built for speed. Launch faster with ready-to-go concepts. Choose a proven product direction, adapt branding, and go to market with minimal R&D time.

Best for:

  • Testing demand with a first product drop
  • Retail and marketplace pilots
  • Brands that want to validate packaging and positioning before moving into private label

Contract manufacturing of pet supplements

Already have a formula and specification? We can act as your pet supplement contract manufacturer: producing at scale with clear QA/QC checkpoints, predictable lead times, stable batch repeatability, and packaging execution.

Best for:

  • Reformulation to improve stability or manufacturing feasibility
  • Scaling production without losing quality consistency
  • Optimizing packaging, shelf-life, and supply chain execution

FAQ

Do you manufacture pet supplements for both dogs and cats?

Yes — dogs and cats are the core focus for this line.

What is the difference between private label and white label pet supplements?

White label is faster with ready-to-go concepts; private label is built around custom formulation and deeper brand differentiation.

Can you act as a pet supplement contract manufacturer if we already have a formula?

Yes. We can manufacture to your spec and help optimize packaging, stability, and batch repeatability.

Do you manufacture soft chews?

Yes — soft chews are available for categories where convenience and palatability are key.

Can you produce oils (e.g., salmon oil for dogs)?

We can support oil-based pet products depending on your packaging and stability requirements, including concepts similar to salmon oil for dogs.
